## Data providers

- Epic Games Public Status and Epic Games Store storefront data are owned and operated by Epic Games.
- Discord and Twitch status data comes from their public official Statuspage endpoints.
- PlayStation and Xbox data comes from their public official status services.
- Roblox status is represented through its public Status.io page/API identifier.
- Steam availability, storefront and tracked-game news come from public Valve/Steam endpoints.
- GamerPower provides giveaway data and requests visible GamerPower.com attribution; every result retains that attribution.
- CheapShark provides public PC-game deal aggregation, commonly in USD.
- GOG catalog data comes from GOG's public catalog endpoint.
- FreeToGame provides the free-to-play game catalog and is visibly attributed.
- Piped provides a no-key representation of public YouTube search. Public instances can throttle or disappear.
- YouTube embeds and thumbnails remain subject to YouTube's terms and each creator's embedding choices.
- `winotify` is installed on Windows under its own license to deliver toast notifications.
